## Executive summary
Extend the existing CMMS v1.2 toward the 184-requirement Pragma On Key / IFS-inspired enterprise baseline, including hospitality facilities and South African compliance administration. The estimate is a budgetary scenario, subject to Gate 0 code audit, client scope and approval. Existing production code and data are not altered by this proposal.

## Deliverables
- Core: assets/locations, work orders, preventive and corrective maintenance, workforce, inventory, procurement, finance, contractor controls, reporting, mobile and integrations.
- Hospitality: hotel, casino, kitchens, conference, electrical distribution, generators, HVAC, water/wastewater, fire and accommodation.
- South Africa: workflows and records to support applicable OHSA/regulations, relevant SANS, POPIA, labour and VAT controls; no representation of statutory certification or legal compliance without independent review.
- AI advisory functions: work order summarisation, fault classification, suggested troubleshooting/checklists, authorised natural-language analytics, recurring-failure insights and report drafting. No autonomous safety permits, electrical isolation, purchasing approval or statutory closure.

## Commercial and AI governance
- Determine whether the project is internally owned product development, commissioned client work or employee-created IP. Confirm source ownership, licensing rights and client data ownership in writing.
- Do not invoice internal R&D or retrospective uncontracted hours as if approved client work. Invoice only authorised milestones or subscriptions through a legitimate contracting entity.
- Confirm tax status and current SARS invoicing/VAT rules before tax invoices are issued. No VAT should be charged unless appropriate.
- AI inputs and outputs: role-based filtering, data minimisation, POPIA review, cross-border processing terms, retention, encryption, model evaluation and human approval for safety-critical or financial decisions.
- Production database, Git history, credentials and media remain unchanged; changes follow reviewed feature branches, staging tests, backup/restore and explicit deployment approval.
- Out of scope unless quoted: unusual third-party licences, statutory certification, legal opinions, major hardware, large historical migration, custom integrations.
